The top 10% of listings by estimated revenue earn 40.7% of total revenue in Chicago and 40.3% in the Twin Cities, but only 36.1% in Columbus, so even though Columbus has more big-host ownership, its earnings are actually spread more evenly across listings.

Being a superhost is worth $43.80 more per night in Chicago and $40.50 more in Columbus, but $1.38 less in the Twin Cities; the superhost badge doesn't pay off the same way everywhere.

Twin Cities entire-home listings bring in a median estimated $19,619 per year, which could be a useful benchmark for a host deciding whether that is worth entering compared to Chicago or Columbus.
